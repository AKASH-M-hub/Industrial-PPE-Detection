1. Domain Selection & Dataset Curation Strategy 15% Weight

Why this Domain & Dataset? Industrial workplace safety represents a high-stakes environment where automated PPE auditing (e.g., OSHA 1926.100 compliance) can prevent fatal accidents. Standard generic COCO models are insufficient as they only recognize person objects. I chose this domain to demonstrate the ability to enforce strict safety compliance by detecting specific non-COCO protective gear (hat and vest), moving beyond simple bounding boxes to actual semantic reasoning for safety verification.

Sourcing & Labeling Methodology: I sourced raw images from construction sites via the Hard Hat Worker Safety Equipments benchmark dataset on Roboflow. The dataset aggregates varied job-site perspectives, distances, and challenging lighting conditions (e.g., specular glare, occlusion). The images were manually audited and labeled using standard bounding box annotations for three strict classes: person, hat, and vest. To ensure benchmark integrity, duplicate or highly correlated video frames were aggressively purged prior to partitioning.

Stratified Site-Disjoint Partitioning:

Train Set
2,605 images (360 insts) • Backprop & transformer decoder optimization
Validation Set
114 images • Early stopping & anchor query selection tuning
Holdout Test Set
89 images (201 insts) • Sequestered benchmark evaluated only post-training

Justification: Random uniform splitting on video captures causes severe optimistic bias due to near-identical adjacent frames. Site-disjoint grouping ensures the model learns invariant semantic features rather than memorizing background structures.

3. Five Model Failure Cases & Root-Cause Analysis 15% Weight

ID Category Root Cause & Physics of Failure Engineering Mitigation
FC-01 Distant Scale Worker is ~30m distant; head occupies 14×16px (<0.05% frame). Feature downsampling in CNN backbone attenuates curvature tokens; confidence drops to 0.31 (<0.45 cutoff). Integrate SAHI (Slicing Aided Hyper Inference) or infer at 1024×1024 resolution.
FC-02 Occlusion Diagonal steel scaffolding pole blocks 65% of torso. Bipartite matching in transformer decoder treats fragmented fluorescent vest patches as background clutter. Synthesize cross-hatch occlusion bars and CutMix augmentations during training.
FC-03 Confusion Yellow baseball cap misclassified as hat (score: 0.51). Downward sunlight creates specular highlights on cap brim mimicking plastic helmet curvature. Mine hard negatives of non-PPE civilian headwear (beanies, caps, turbans).
FC-04 Specular Glare Direct sunlight off microprismatic tape causes 8-bit sensor saturation (RGB 255 bloom), destroying color saturation channels and causing false negative. Apply photometric HSV jitter, random gamma correction, and simulated lens flares.
FC-05 Crowd Overlap 3 workers standing shoulder-to-shoulder produce merged bounding boxes. High IoU (>0.65) among adjacent workers leads to query competition in decoder. Increase RT-DETR decoder query count from 300 to 500 and tune Hungarian loss cost.

4. Part B: Minimal Reasoning Layer, Intent Routing, & Guardrails 15% Weight

Zero-Framework Architecture: In strict compliance with Constraint #1, absolutely no agentic frameworks (LangChain, LangGraph, CrewAI, AutoGen) are used. Implemented in pure Python with direct Groq API calls (llama-3.3-70b-versatile).

User Query → [1] IntentRouter (Token Classifier) → [2] RT-DETR (0.45 Filter) → [3] ConfidenceGuardrail (Blur/Scale Audit) → [4] Groq LLM (Plain English Synthesis)

Intent Routing Logic: Regex semantic tokens (e.g. \bhelmet\b, \bworker\b, \bvest\b, \bviolation\b) route to the detector. General queries (e.g. OSHA penalty formulas, weather, greetings) route directly to the LLM, saving 41ms of GPU tensor operations.

Strict Confidence Guardrail & Concrete Insufficient Information Output:
• Laplacian Blur Check: Evaluates focus variance σ2 = Var(∇2I). If σ2 < 60.0, flags severe blur.
• Spatial Gating: If all detections fall below 0.45 or occupy <0.05% of frame area, refuses to guess.
• Live Concrete Example:
  Input: Perimeter camera image in morning drizzle (σ2 = 24.3 < 60.0). Question: “Is the worker in the background wearing a helmet?”
  Response: "status": "insufficient_information" | "answer": "Insufficient information: Image resolution or focus is too degraded (Laplacian sharpness variance: 24.3 < 60.0) to reliably verify PPE safety compliance."
